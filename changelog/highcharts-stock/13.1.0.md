# Changelog for Highcharts Stock v13.1.0 (2026-09-18)

- Most changes listed under Highcharts 13.1.0 above also apply to Highcharts Stock 13.1.0.

## Upgrade notes
- The standalone navigator's `chart` option was replaced with `chartOptions`, which accepts a general chart options object. Chart options previously nested under `chart.chart` must be moved to `chartOptions.chart`.

## Bug fixes
- Fixed [#25335](https://github.com/highcharts/highcharts/issues/25335), excessive processing and DOM traffic on `chart.update`.
- Fixed [#24715](https://github.com/highcharts/highcharts/issues/24715), standalone navigator ignored chart and navigator height.
